One item per line. Works with questions, definitions, formulas, or plain notes. Add #subject anywhere on a line to force a subject (e.g. #bio), and ! at the start to mark it high priority.

How it decides

  • Subject — matches keywords against a built-in dictionary (math, biology, chemistry, physics, history, English, computer science, economics, geography).
  • Type — question, formula, definition, date/event, or note.
  • Priority — lines starting with !, or things it can already explain in depth, get pushed up.
  • Explained? — green if the built-in library has a full explanation, amber if it doesn't yet.
